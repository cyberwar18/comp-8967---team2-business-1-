- Event permits: an event in a public place (a park, a street) needs a permit upload (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB). Organizer approves, then Neighbr staff approve the permit, then the event becomes a map pin until it ends. A hall or an online event needs no permit.

- Senders get a notification for every decision (permit approve, decline, ask; report outcomes).
